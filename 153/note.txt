- Since the array is sorted and rotated, the minimum element is the point where the rotation happened.

- Compare nums[mid] with nums[right] to determine which half contains the minimum:
    - If nums[mid] > nums[right], the minimum must be to the right of mid, so left = mid + 1.
    - Otherwise, the minimum is at mid or to its left, so right = mid.

Continue until left == right. Both pointers will point to the minimum element.

Time Complexity: O(log n)
Space Complexity: O(1)
