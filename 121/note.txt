We need to buy the stock first and sell it later to get the maximum profit.

Go through the prices from left to right. While doing this, always remember the
lowest price we have seen so far because buying at the lowest possible price gives us 
the best chance of making more profit.

For every current price, imagine selling the stock today:
    profit = current price - lowest price seen so far

If this profit is greater than our previous maximum profit, update maxProfit.

After checking the profit, update lowestPrice if the current price is cheaper.
This cheaper price might give us a better profit on a future day.

Example: [7, 1, 5, 3, 6, 4]
When we reach 6, the lowest price seen before it is 1, so selling at 6 gives a profit of 5.

We only need to traverse the array once.

Time: O(n)
Space: O(1)
