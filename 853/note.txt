Car Fleet Logic

- Calculate the time each car would take to reach the target.
- Sort cars by their starting positions.
- Traverse from right to left, starting with the car closest to the target.
- Keep track of the maximum time seen so far (maxTime).

- If a car behind takes more time than maxTime, it cannot catch the fleet ahead,
  so it forms a new fleet.

- Otherwise, it catches up with the fleet ahead and becomes part of that same fleet.

Key idea: A slower car ahead determines the time of its entire fleet. Only cars with a
greater arrival time than all cars ahead create a new fleet.
